Vracker wheel markers

Print at 100% (A4), cut out each disc, and stick it on something that spins freely (cardboard, a jar lid, a fidget spinner base). Each wheel has its own color pair: wheel A is GREEN center + MAGENTA pointer, wheel B is CYAN center + YELLOW pointer. That is how the tracker tells the players apart - do not mix the colors between wheels. Keep both dots of a wheel visible to the camera.

Printing in black and white will NOT work - the tracker finds the dots by color. If your printer's greens/magentas differ a lot, open the app and use "sample center/pointer color" to calibrate.
