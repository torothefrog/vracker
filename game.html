SPACE DODGERS

One screen, endless asteroids. Dodge the rocks, catch the stars, and keep your five hearts as long as you can.

1 PLAYER - one ship: any wheel, A/D or arrow keys, or drag anywhere.
2 PLAYERS - Ship A turns with the green wheel (or A/D keys, or drag the left half), Ship B with the blue wheel (or arrow keys, or the right half).

Camera stays on this device - nothing is uploaded. Hold the wheels level and pick a mode (or press "recenter wheels") so straight ahead is straight ahead.

GAME OVER

Enter / Space also restarts.
