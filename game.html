SPACE DODGERS

Two ships, one screen, endless asteroids. Dodge the rocks, catch the stars, and keep your five hearts as long as you can.

Ship A turns with the green wheel (or A/D keys, or drag the left half). Ship B turns with the blue wheel (or ←/→ keys, or drag the right half).

Camera stays on this device - nothing is uploaded. Hold both wheels level and press START (or "recenter wheels") so straight ahead is straight ahead.
